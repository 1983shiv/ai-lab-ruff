# Acme Gadgets Help Center

## Refunds
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in the original box. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item. Gift cards and downloadable software cannot be refunded.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 4.99 dollars. Orders over 50 dollars ship free. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 14.99 dollars. We ship to the US and Canada only.

## Warranty
All Acme devices have a 1 year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Water damage and cracked screens are not covered. To make a claim, contact support with your order number and a photo of the problem.

## Account and password
To reset your password, click Forgot password on the login page and enter your email. The reset link expires after 24 hours. If you do not get the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Eastern Time. Weekend messages are answered on Monday. Phone support is available for premium customers only.
